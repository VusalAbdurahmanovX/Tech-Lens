# Technology marks

Icons are bundled locally; the extension makes no network requests for logos.

- SVG brand marks: Simple Icons 16.0.0, https://www.npmjs.com/package/simple-icons/v/16.0.0 (CC0; LICENSE.md).
- Adobe Fonts: Simple Icons 11.0.0, https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@11.0.0/icons/adobefonts.svg (CC0).
- Microsoft Clarity: official favicon, https://claritystatic.azureedge.net/images/logo.ico
- reCAPTCHA: official asset, https://www.gstatic.com/recaptcha/api2/logo_48.png
- AOS: text wordmark matching the project's text-based heading, https://michalsnik.github.io/aos/ (not a separate pictorial logo).
- Google tag, Meta Pixel and Cloudflare Turnstile use their respective parent-brand marks.

Brand names and marks identify detected technologies; they do not imply endorsement.
The Simple Icons CC0 license does not cover third-party trademarks or the official assets listed separately.
